A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity is written in different ways. This checklist shows how to read it on a listing before you compare two units.

Read the stated size

The register lists these sizes in the titles: P01 '6 Quart' (with '2 Independent 3Qt' baskets), P02 '26QT', P03 '8 Qt'. Each is the seller's wording. P01 gives a per-basket figure in its title; the total is not stated there.

Ask whether the number is total or per basket

A dual-basket unit can be listed by total size or by each basket. Compare like with like, and check the listing's product information table for the figure the seller uses.

Limits of this guide

No cooking test was run. The register does not hold dimensions, so this guide does not compare physical footprint.

Checklist

  • Write down how many people you usually cook for
  • Compare the stated quart figure from the same listing type (basket vs oven)
  • Check whether the figure is total or per basket
  • Read the product information table on the live listing for dimensions, which this register does not hold
