How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

Dishwasher-safe parts save time, but they do not tell you how much counter the unit takes up. Here is how to check both.

Cleanup as the listings describe it

P01's title says 'Dishwasher Safe Frying Baskets'. P03's title says 'XL Nonstick Dishwasher-Safe Basket'. P02's title says 'Six Cooking Accessories Included', but the register does not say which parts are dishwasher-safe.

Counter space

No dimension or weight data was captured for any register row. Counter space therefore cannot be compared here. Use the listing's product information table, and record the figure with its date.

Trade-offs to note

A larger unit may hold more food and take more counter. A unit with more accessories may have more parts to wash. Weigh both against how often you would use it.

Checklist

  • Which parts the listing says are dishwasher-safe
  • How many removable parts there are
  • The unit's dimensions from the product information table (not in the register)
  • Where the unit would sit and what you would move to make room
