What was built

  • Shop-first front page — products and guides above the fold; all dashboard surfaces live in one footer group (Home).
  • Five question-shaped guides with inline disclosure, sponsored CTAs, and per-guide source lists (Guides).
  • Verification surfaces — shelf + intent register, field notes, flags, open questions, suggestions review with a records-only path chooser.
  • Measurement — five-event privacy-preserving counter (assets/analytics.js), self-hosted endpoint code (tools/analytics_endpoint.py), empty campaign ledger (data/campaign-ledger.csv).
  • Findability — sitemap.xml, robots.txt, RSS feed, client-side search, canonical/OG tags, JSON-LD (WebSite / BlogPosting / ItemList).
  • The gate — scripts/check_site.py enforces disclosure placement, sponsored+tagged links, source coverage on every data-table row, internal link integrity, sitemap/feed coverage, and data↔page sync before any push.

What is deliberately absent

  • Commission rates (blank + unverified — schedule requires sign-in; FLG-004).
  • Product ratings (not captured from listings' own rating blocks; SG-17).
  • Any conversion, revenue, or demand claim (no authorized first-party data exists; Priority 4).
  • Email capture forms (provider + privacy decision pending; Q-14).
  • Hotlinked product imagery (only official Program Content after account approval; Q-11).
