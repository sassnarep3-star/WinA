FLG-002 High

All five submitted tokens are 9 characters; Amazon's official ASIN definition is 10 characters

Amazon's Creators API documents ASIN as a '10-Character alphanumeric unique identifier assigned by Amazon'. Each submitted token is 9 characters, so none of the tokens is itself an ASIN. Direct /dp/<token> fetches did not render a product page (two independent tokens tested); the /dp/<10-char ASIN> control renders normally.

Status: open — recorded as observation; no claim that the short links are invalid (see FLG-003)

Sources: S11 — Amazon Associates Creators API — GetItems operation (ItemIdT · S18 — Direct fetch of /dp/B04G1MEwY (submitted token used as ASIN) · S19 — Direct fetch of /dp/B0f3GKalj (submitted token used as ASIN)

FLG-003 High

Compliance status of the supplied link.amazon/* short links is unresolved

The brief forbids claiming the supplied short links are invalid, and their exact status is unresolved here. What is verified: each token redirects to a live official listing (observed 2026-11-20). What is missing: whether the link.amazon domain and format qualify as a 'Special Link' under Operating Agreement §1 (tagged link formats Amazon provides), and whether the operator of the link.amazon domain is Amazon. Required action: test one link per product in the enrolled Associates workflow, confirm destination and tracking report, then scale (S02 of the brief). Until then the site links to official /dp/ listing URLs with the owner-supplied tracking tag and rel="sponsored".

Status: open — one-link-per-product Special Link test required
