Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

By WinA EditorialPublished 2026-11-20Updated 2026-11-20

⚠ Working title — an editorial hypothesis, not a claim about popularity, search volume, or product superiority.

Multi-function appliances sell on the word “more.” Before you buy one, run it through questions that cannot be answered by a preset count. Twelve of them, in the order we ask them.

The questions

  1. What will it cook in a normal week? Write three meals. If none of them need the extra functions, the functions are packaging.
  2. What does it replace? A 26 qt french-door unit that replaces the toaster oven and the air fryer earns its footprint twice. One that replaces nothing earns it once.
  3. What does it NOT replace? Honest answer now beats regret later — nothing on our shelf replaces a full-size oven for a holiday bird; check cavity size on the listing before assuming.
  4. How many presets will you actually press? The Emeril listing states “24 One-Touch Preset Options.” Pick the two you would use weekly. If you cannot name two, presets are not the reason to buy.
  5. Who washes it? Parts count beats function count — see the cleanup guide.
  6. Where does it live permanently? Tape the footprint. If the tape competes with your coffee station, something will lose.
  7. What is the failure question? What breaks first in your home: coating, latch, window, element? Choose the listing whose headline features sit furthest from your known weak point.
  8. What does the return window look like? Check the listing's current return terms on the day you buy; they change seasonally (the listings we read on 2026-11-20 advertised holiday return windows).
  9. What is the ceiling price you will not exceed? Set it before you see the upsell. Our observed buy-box prices for the shelf: $69.99, $79.93, $149.99 that day.
  10. Do you need wireless, app, or WiFi at all? The CHEF iQ Sense lists WiFi unlimited range; a $16.99-class instant-read solves the same core problem without an account. Decide the layer you want to live with.
  11. What does “multi-function” cost in preheat and storage? Modes share one cavity — check the listing's stated time and temperature ranges for the modes you named in Q1.
  12. Would you buy it again after month three? That is the only review that matters, and you cannot read it yet. Buy the version whose spec sheet you can defend without a review score.
Presets are cheap. Counter space, washing-up, and the honest answer to “what does it replace” are the real price.
