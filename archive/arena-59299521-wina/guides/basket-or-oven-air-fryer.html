Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Published 2026-11-20 · Category: air fryers · Estimated read: 5 min

The first question most buyers answer before looking at features is the form factor: basket-style or oven-style. This guide frames that decision as a set of comparisons — counter footprint, basket capacity per quart claim, shake-versus-tray workflow, and what fits on your counter after it is unboxed — rather than declaring one style better.
