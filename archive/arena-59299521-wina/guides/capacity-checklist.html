A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Published 2026-11-20 · Category: air fryers · Estimated read: 5 min

“6 quart” does not mean six quarts of usable cooking space in every design. This guide is a checklist for reading capacity claims skeptically: total quart vs per-basket quart, basket shape and usable depth, dual-basket sync workflows, and how much food actually fits for the number of people you cook for.

This guide does not: cite search volume, rank products, or claim that any capacity is best for any household. Product mentions are illustrative, drawn from our verified register, and every outbound link is marked sponsored.

Checklist: what to compare before buying

  • Total advertised quart vs quart per basket — are you comparing like for like?
  • Basket dimensions and usable depth, not just the volume number on the box
  • Single vs dual basket: can you cook two foods at once with synchronized finish?
  • How much counter depth the basket needs when pulled out
  • Typical meals per basket size for your household size
