Executive summary

WinA is an AI-powered Amazon affiliate marketing platform — built here as a polished, publication-style marketing site — that automates product research, identifies trending and high-converting products, analyzes pricing/reviews/competition/commission potential, generates SEO-optimized articles, comparisons, and buying guides, publishes and manages affiliate links, tracks clicks and conversions, and continuously optimizes campaigns. Automation, scalability, data-driven decisions, and Amazon Associates / FTC compliance are prioritized.
