Suggestions — review before implementing

A dedicated section for suggestions. Someone reviews each suggestion and chooses the best path before any change is implemented. Nothing here goes live without review. These suggestions feed into the prompt that designs the site: a polished, trendy marketing affiliate website that earns organic traffic — not AI slop.

How to use this section

1. Review each suggestion below. 2. Choose the best path (adopt, adapt, defer, or decline). 3. Record the decision and the reason in the decision log. 4. Only then implement. Suggestions that improve decision-making (clearer comparisons, better questions, honest specs) are prioritized over suggestions that add noise.

Design & editorial suggestions

Catchy, trendy article topics (seasonal rotation)

Rotate seasonal, question-led topics tied to real cooking moments: “Best air fryer for a small kitchen”, “Counter-depth vs. basket: which fits?”, “Holiday grilling: instant-read vs. wireless”. Each topic must still be a genuine buyer question with verified specs — trend never overrides truth.

Organic traffic plan (findable)

Publish SEO-optimized comparisons and buying guides on a weekly cadence; add each to sitemap and RSS. One site to index, one reason to rank (useful answers). Use measured queries — not instinct — to pick the next topic. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console (SG-29).

Visual brand & imagery (read like a publication)

Consistent warm editorial brand, clean dashboard-like shop UI, single navigation group, authorship + dates on every guide, seasonal hero themes. No Amazon product images (IP License) — styled category visuals instead. Keep every dashboard surface reachable from the footer.

Compliance gate in publishing workflow

Keep the exact Associates statement visible; review disclosure placement and endorsement context against the Operating Agreement and FTC guidance before publishing. Gate: disclosure above the fold, sponsored marking, no prices/ratings/images without an approved API. S01 · S07 · S24.

Measurement before optimization (Priority 4)

Start with an empty campaign ledger; add timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks, and reported orders only from authorized first-party reports. Dated provenance trail only. No conversion score or revenue forecast. Configure the self-hosted analytics endpoint first.

Avoid AI slop (human-sounding, sourced)

Write human-sounding buyer questions first. Every product claim ties to a source; unverified values stay blank and flagged. Author byline and dates on every guide. If a claim cannot be sourced, it does not ship.
