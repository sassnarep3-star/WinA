Click and conversion measurement

Measurement

Five events, defined before any content was written, recorded without cookies or identifiers — and currently switched off, because no collector is deployed.

Status: not recording

No collector endpoint deployed. Client is inert rather than silently failing. The client checks a config flag at load; with no endpoint it returns immediately and nothing is sent anywhere. Nothing is buffered, queued, or forwarded to a third party.

The five events

Defined before the guides were written, so that the questions come before the answers.

Event definitions and payloads. Every payload is deliberately minimal: no cookies, no identifiers, no referrer, no personal data.
IDEventFires whenWhat it recordsWhat question it answers
EV-01 page_view Once per page load, after the page is visible. path only — no query string, no referrer, no identifier Which guides and products are read at all.
EV-02 outbound_click On click of any element marked data-affiliate. asin, page path, position on page The one number that matters most: which product earns the click, from which page.
EV-03 guide_read When a guide reaches 75% scroll depth, once per page load. guide slug Whether a question-shaped guide is actually read or just landed on.
EV-04 compare_view On load of the comparison table page. page path Whether readers want side-by-side or narrative.
EV-05 capture_intent On submit attempt of the reader capture form. page path, form location Whether anyone wants a digest, before investing in an ESP.

Why the collector is self-hosted

Adding a third-party analytics script would mean handing a vendor a record of reader behaviour on a page about buying things, in exchange for a dashboard. The reader gets nothing from that trade. A first-party collector on a small worker keeps the data in one place and keeps the payloads minimal.

The stated principles are in the code and on the privacy page:

  • No cookies, no fingerprinting, no cross-site identifiers.
  • No personal data leaves the browser. The capture form never transmits an address.
  • Raw events are not retained beyond 30 days; only daily aggregates persist.
  • The counter is first-party and self-hosted, so no third-party processor receives reader behaviour.

What cannot be measured from here

Orders and revenue. Only Amazon can report those, and only to the account holder, inside the Associates dashboard. There is no API path from this site to that number. When a report is exported, its figures are entered by hand into the campaign ledger with the report date attached. Nothing is inferred from clicks, and no click-to-order conversion rate is estimated.

Whether the tracking tag is working at all. Our counter can record that a reader clicked. Only Amazon can confirm the click was attributed to shopnow047c-20. Those are different facts and are stored separately.

Deploying it

The collector is in _worker/ and is a single small handler with no third-party dependency. Deploy it, set the endpoint in the build config, rebuild, and the flag flips. The exact steps are in the repository README.

Until that happens, this page is a plan rather than a report. It is marked as such deliberately, because a measurement page that implies data exists when it does not is worse than no measurement page.
