Why it is on this list

  • 26 quarts is the largest capacity in this set by a wide margin — it takes a whole chicken or a full sheet of vegetables in one pass.
  • French doors open outward instead of dropping down, so you can reach in with both hands without clearing the counter in front.
  • 24 one-touch presets cover the modes people actually use nightly, which shortens the learning curve for a big appliance.
  • Six cooking accessories are included, so the accessories line item is already in the box.

What to think twice about

  • This is a countertop oven that also air-fries. If you want crisp fries in small batches fast, a basket model heats up quicker.
  • Footprint and height are the real constraint. Confirm them against your space before ordering — we could not verify dimensions from an official source.
  • More surface area and more accessories means more washing per meal than a single drawer.

Who it is wrong for

Anyone cooking two portions on a weeknight. It is a small oven, and it behaves like one: more to preheat, more to wash, more counter permanently occupied. If you want fast fries for two, this is the wrong tool and you will resent it by the third week.
