A fast way to narrow it down

Cooking for one or two, want crispy texture on schedule? → start with the two basket models (Chefman 6QT dual / TurboFry 8QT).

Cooking for a crowd, need oven volume or a second oven? → the Emeril 26QT is the only oven-style unit here.

Just want to stop overcooking things? → the ThermoMaven covers spot-checks for the price of a pizza; the CHEF iQ adds hands-off monitoring for long cooks.
