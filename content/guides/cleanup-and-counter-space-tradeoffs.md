## Cleanup depends on what touches the food

Baskets, trays, racks, and the cooking chamber all collect grease and crumbs. The question is how easily each part comes clean.

- **Look for "dishwasher safe" in the listing.** Two titles in our set include the phrase: {{product:P-01}} ("Dishwasher Safe Frying Baskets") and {{product:P-03}} ("XL Nonstick Dishwasher-Safe Basket"). The title for {{product:P-02}} does not mention dishwasher safety, so check its accessory list before you assume anything.
- **Count the parts.** The {{product:P-02}} title says "Six Cooking Accessories Included." Six parts can mean six things to wash.
- **Check how the nonstick surface is described.** Both {{product:P-01}} and {{product:P-03}} list a "Nonstick" basket. The listings do not say how long the coating lasts, and no coating claim is verified here.

## Counter space is more than the footprint

- **Footprint:** the width and depth of the base.
- **Clearance:** room to pull out a basket, or to open a door fully. Basket units need space to slide the drawer out. Oven-style units need room for the door.
- **Storage:** whether it fits in a cabinet when you are not using it.
- **Venting:** where it can release heat. Follow the manual for minimum clearances.

## A simple test before you buy

1. Measure the spot: width, depth, and height under any cabinet.
2. Find the unit's dimensions in the listing's product details. Our check did not capture them, so do not assume a figure.
3. Compare the two, leaving room for the door or drawer.
