## More presets are not the same as more use

The {{product:P-02}} listing says "Multi-Function" and "24 One-Touch Preset Options." Presets are useful only if you cook the food they are built for. Before you pay for a long list of functions, ask these questions.

1. **Which three things will I cook most often?** Check that the unit has a setting or method for each one in its manual.
2. **Does it replace something I already own?** A toaster, an oven, or a basket fryer may already do the job. Or it may simply add to the counter.
3. **What is in the box?** The {{product:P-02}} title says six accessories are included. Count them against the things you would really use.
4. **How hard is it to clean?** See the [cleanup guide](../cleanup-and-counter-space-tradeoffs/).
5. **What does the warranty cover?** Read the warranty section of the listing and the manufacturer's page. We did not capture warranty terms.
6. **Which certifications are claimed?** Look for named certifications, then check them with the certifying body. We did not verify any certification claim on this site.
7. **Where will it sit?** Use the [counter-space checklist](../cleanup-and-counter-space-tradeoffs/#counter-space-is-more-than-the-footprint).

## Let the answers narrow the list

If your answers point to one or two jobs, a simpler appliance may suit you better. If they point to several, a multi-function unit may make sense. Either way, check the listing and the manual for the details that matter to you.
