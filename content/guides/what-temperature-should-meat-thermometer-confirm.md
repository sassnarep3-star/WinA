## Start with the food, not the thermometer

A meat thermometer is only useful if you know the number to aim for. The USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service publishes minimum internal temperatures for each food. Its guidance says to cook all food to these temperatures, "as measured with a food thermometer, before removing food from the heat source."

| Food | Minimum internal temperature | Rest time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beef, pork, veal and lamb steaks, chops and roasts | 145°F | At least 3 minutes | [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) |
| Ground meats | 160°F | Not stated | [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) |
| All poultry, including ground poultry | 165°F | Not stated | [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) |
| Fish and shellfish | 145°F | Not stated | [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) |
| Eggs | 160°F | Not stated | [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) |
| Leftovers | 165°F | Not stated | [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) |
| Fresh or smoked ham, uncooked | 145°F | At least 3 minutes | [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) |

The chart also lists reheating temperatures for fully cooked ham. The page is the reference, so use it for the full list.

## What the chart does not cover

The chart does not say where to place a probe, how often to check calibration, or how to read a wireless display. Follow the thermometer's own instructions for those.
