## What this site is

The Countertop Guide is an independent buying guide for two kitchen categories: air fryers and meat thermometers. Every guide is written from listing text, labelled manufacturer claims, and official references. The site earns commission when readers buy through the Amazon links, and the disclosure is on every page.

## How a guide is written

1. We start with a buyer question, not a product.
2. We read the listing titles and the official sources the guide cites. We record each source in the [source register](../sources/).
3. We label any manufacturer claim as a claim, and we do not turn it into a fact without a source.
4. We state what we did not check. We do not claim hands-on testing.
5. A reviewer checks the disclosure placement and the sources before publication.
