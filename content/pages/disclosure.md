## The short version

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Links marked **Sponsored** go to Amazon. If you buy through one, the site may earn a commission.

## What we mark

- Every Amazon product link carries `rel="sponsored"` ([Google's guidance on paid links, SRC-09](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links)) and is labelled **Sponsored** where it appears.
- Links to official sources, such as Amazon policy pages and government guidance, are not sponsored links.

## Why this statement is here

Amazon's Associates Operating Agreement requires the statement "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." to be stated clearly and prominently on the site ([SRC-01, §5](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement)). It appears under the masthead on every page and again in the footer.

The FTC says a disclosure is likely to be missed if it appears only on an About Me or profile page, at the end of a post, or anywhere that requires a person to click to see more. It should be placed so it is hard to miss ([SRC-05](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)). That is why the statement sits above the content on each page, not only here.

## What we do not claim

- **No hands-on testing.** We have not tested the products on this site. Guides cite listing text and labelled manufacturer claims.
- **No prices or star ratings.** Participation Requirements §2(b) allows prices and availability only through an Amazon-served link or the Product Advertising API, and §6(t) allows reviews and star ratings only through the API ([SRC-02](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies)).
- **No rankings or "best" claims.** Each guide compares features that the listings state.
- **No revenue forecast.** The site does not predict earnings.
