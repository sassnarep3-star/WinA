Why this is empty

There is no traffic history and no Associates report for this site, because it launched with this build. Any number placed here would be invented. A conversion score or revenue forecast built on nothing is worse than no score at all, because it looks like evidence.

What makes a row admissible

Rules for adding a ledger row. A row missing any of these is not entered.
ColumnRule
dateThe date of the report, not the date you typed it in.
sourceWhere the traffic came from — organic, direct, referral, by name.
channelThe specific channel or campaign if one was tagged. Otherwise the same as source.
eligibleSessionsFrom the associated report only. Not estimated from page views.
clicksOutbound clicks to Amazon. Our own counter may differ from Amazon's; record both if they disagree, and note it.
reportedOrdersOrders as reported by Amazon. Never inferred from commission, and never modelled.
provenanceThe specific report and its date range. Without this the row is a rumour.
