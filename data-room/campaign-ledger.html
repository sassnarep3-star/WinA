Why an empty ledger is a feature

The brief for this project asks for evidence-based iteration: measure before automating optimization. An empty, well-defined ledger makes "we don't know yet" visible and keeps future numbers honest. When gates G-4 and G-6 close, the first authorized rows land here with their provenance — and only then do proposals that depend on data (S-02, S-05, S-10, S-13) unlock.
