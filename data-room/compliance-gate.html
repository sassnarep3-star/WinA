Compliance gate

Automate what can be automated; gate what cannot. Every publishing change passes the three automated gates below (they run in CI and block broken or undisclosed pages). The four human gates carry named checklists and explicit owner actions.

Checklist G-4 · Associates link test (owner action O-1)

  1. Sign in to Associates Central for the account owning shopnow047c-20; confirm the tracking ID is active.
  2. For one product first: generate a SiteStripe/API Special Link, click it in a logged-out browser, and confirm it lands on the intended listing (compare with the intent record row).
  3. Check the Associates Reports "clicks" for the test; screenshot and file it as provenance.
  4. Repeat for the other four; then fill the intent record's "Official listing URL" + "Confirmation date" columns.
  5. Only then scale link placements (per the brief: test one per product before scaling).

Checklist G-5 · disclosure & endorsement review (owner action O-2)

  1. Open home, shop, and one guide on mobile and desktop. Confirm the disclosure strip is visible before the first product link without scrolling past it (FTC "clear and conspicuous", FAQ).
  2. Confirm every sponsored link is labeled in words ("sponsored"/"commission") next to the link, not just in the header.
  3. Confirm nothing on the site implies we tested products we haven't, or states opinions we don't hold (16 CFR 255.1 honesty principles, eCFR).
  4. Re-read the Associates Participation Requirements (identification as an associate, prohibited content) and confirm the site text matches.
  5. Date and sign the review here; repeat after any disclosure-text change.

Checklist G-6 · analytics endpoint (owner action O-3)

Follow analytics/README.md: deploy worker.js with the KV binding, POST a test event, verify the KV day bucket, then set the cc-endpoint meta value. Expect the first ledger rows within a week (provenance: "cc-collect").

Checklist G-7 · automation approval (owner action O-4-a)

Before any scheduled price/stock checker or API client runs: confirm the tooling route complies with the Associates Operating Agreement and the Product Advertising API policies where applicable. Record the decision and date here. Until then, re-observation is manual and prices remain dated snapshots.
