Methodology & provenance trail

How the facts were obtained, what was deliberately not done, and the dated trail behind every number.

Method

  1. Link resolution (2026-11-20). Each submitted link.amazon/… URL was fetched; the final canonical page was recorded in the intent record, in fields kept separate from the submitted identifiers.
  2. Fact extraction (2026-11-20). For each canonical listing: page title, category breadcrumbs, buy-box price/list price/stock/seller, the rating block (score + count), the product-details table, and "About this item" bullets. Values were transcribed only where they appeared verbatim.
  3. Policy sources (2026-11-20). The FTC Endorsement Guides pages, the eCFR text of 16 CFR Part 255, and the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and Program Policies were read before their citation on the site.
  4. Blank-and-flag rule. Any cell not confirmed from an official page was left blank and marked unverified. No value was inferred, completed, or copied from third-party roundups.
  5. Contradiction rule. Where a source contradicts itself (FLG-004, FLG-007), both readings are published with the flag — never silently harmonized.
  6. Automation & gate. tools/check_site.py re-verifies structure (disclosures, rel tags, source rows, link integrity) on every change; human gates are listed on the compliance page.

What was deliberately not done

  • No products were purchased or physically tested — no performance claims appear anywhere.
  • No submitted identifier was "corrected" to a canonical ASIN; observed targets live in separate fields.
  • No conversion score, revenue forecast, or traffic estimate was produced.
  • No third-party review content was copied or paraphrased as our own.
  • No automated scraping of Amazon runs today (gate G-7 must approve any future automation).

Refresh policy

Listing facts are snapshots stamped "observed 2026-11-20." Prices, star ratings, review counts, stock states and offer details are read for verification but are not displayed on the site (Associates IP License §2(b); Participation Requirements §6(t) — see FLG-044). The ledger stays empty until first-party reports exist. When gate G-4 closes, the intent record gets its confirmation columns; when proposal S-10 is approved and gate G-7 is cleared, re-observation becomes scheduled and every refreshed value re-stamps its date. Corrections from readers go through the repository issue tracker and are logged in the table above.
