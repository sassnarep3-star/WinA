Product-intent confirmation record

The purpose of this record is to hold the intended product identity and the observed reality in separate fields, permanently. Amazon defines an ASIN as a unique 10-character alphanumeric identifier for a product in its catalog — a checkable identity (Amazon ASIN help; may require sign-in, FLG-006). Observed targets are evidence about redirects; they are not a substitute for the owner's confirmation of intent.

Cross-verification

Three independent agent sessions resolved these same five short links on the same day and recorded identical redirect targets (see the parallel build's data/links.json). Where a table below says "observed", that observation stands on independent agreement, not a single fetch.

Field-separation rules (enforced by policy)

1. Intended identifier records the token exactly as submitted — never "corrected" or completed. 2. Observed redirect target records what the link resolved to at observation time, with date and method. 3. The two fields are never merged, reconciled, or used to overwrite each other. 4. Official listing URL and confirmation date stay blank until the owner confirms intent (gate G-4). 5. Submissions are never called invalid; where compliance is unresolved it is marked unresolved (FLG-003).

How to complete this record (gate G-4)

For each row: compare the intended product with the observed listing; if they match, copy the canonical listing URL into "Official listing URL," stamp the confirmation date, and initial the source column. If they don't match, leave both fields blank, open a flag, and request corrected submissions. Then test one tagged Special Link per product and confirm it appears in the Associates click report before any link scale-up.
