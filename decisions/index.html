How to use this page

Review workflow: every suggestion has one row on /decisions/ with its current state and, once decided, the date, the decision and the reason. Nothing is implemented on the strength of a suggestion alone.

  1. Answer the blocking questions first (Q-04 enrolment, Q-09 testing, Q-16 measurement).
  2. Score each suggestion: impact × confidence ÷ cost.
  3. Read the risk column before accepting; a suggestion that needs a credential is gated, not blocked.
  4. Accept or reject in writing, with a one-line reason and a date.
  5. Re-review quarterly against measured data, not instinct.

What would make us drop a suggestion

  • A topic with no measured demand after four weeks is dropped, not padded.
  • A suggestion whose risk has grown (for example it would breach policy) is moved to rejected.
  • No new topic is added from instinct while the query report is empty.
