Getting in touch, and where not to

A deliberately short page, because one of the rules of being an Amazon Associate is that you must not act as Amazon's customer service — and this site would rather explain that than leave an inbox to rot.

Order, delivery, return or refund questions

Those have to go to Amazon, through the contact directions on the Amazon site you used. We cannot see your order, we cannot change it, and under the Associates agreement we must not take on handling Amazon customer contacts. Anything you send us about an order will get you a link back to Amazon and nothing else.

Corrections, and “you got this wrong”

The most useful message this site can receive, because the register is a file and a fix is an edit. Every page's claims trace to /verify/; if a quotation does not match the source, say which row and what the live page says.

Product owners and brands

We do not accept review units, gifted products, sponsored placements or paid inclusion, and we do not remove factual content on request. If a page states something about your product that is not in your own published documentation, the correction we can make is to the source.
