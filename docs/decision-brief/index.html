Decision brief

The 14 questions that gate every decision here

A decision is only as good as the information behind it, so this page is the information: what we hold, what we do not, where each missing piece actually lives, and the default this build took while it waited. 3 of these are blocking; the rest change the plan rather than the legality of it.

The board these feed

What we know without asking anyone

  • Amazon's own definition of an ASIN is a 10-character alphanumeric code, and none of the five submitted identifiers is 10 characters.
  • A qualifying link has to carry the Associates tag as a URL parameter, in a format Amazon provides — not as a wrapper page or a redirect.
  • Disclosure in the exact words of the Operating Agreement has to be clear and prominent, and a breach of it is a material breach.
  • The FTC standard for a material connection is that it be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, where people will see it, not on a separate page.
  • Amazon reviews an application only once at least three qualifying sales land, personal orders excluded, and expects a site's content to be recent — with roughly 10 posts as its own rule of thumb.
  • PA-API access, and therefore any automated product data, exists only for an accepted account whose primary owner signed up for it.
Q-01

What are the five real ASINs, and what are the products?

gates a decision
Why it matters
Everything monetizable, and every product claim on the site, waits on this. The supplied values are 9 characters and cannot resolve to a listing.
What we already have
Five strings and a non-Amazon host, plus a niche hypothesis from the editorial titles in the brief.
What is missing
10-character ASINs or the /dp/ URLs, product names, and the marketplace.
Where the answer lives
Open each product page and copy the ten characters after /dp/ — or search the product in Associates Central and use the link builder (affiliate-program.amazon.com/home/productlinks/search).
Answer format
value (give me the literal value)

If nobody answers

Q-04

Which marketplace do the links point at?

shapes the plan
Why it matters
ASINs are marketplace-specific; amazon.com and amazon.co.uk can carry different ASINs for the same box, and commission categories differ by locale.

The -20 suffix implies a US Store ID, so the config assumes www.amazon.com.

Confirmation, and the marketplace for each listing.
Where the answer lives
The listing URL you copied the ASIN from; and Associates Central → Account Settings for the account's marketplace.

Fastest way to answer all of it

  1. Log into Associates Central: note the Store ID, the Tracking IDs, the registered sites, and whether the account is accepted. That answers Q-02, Q-03 and half of Q-05 in one sitting.
  2. Open each of the five real listings and copy the ten characters after /dp/ into data/products.json. Q-01 and Q-06, done.
  3. Build one tagged link in the link builder, click it logged out, confirm the destination and that a click appears in reporting. That is the whole test in SG-25.
  4. Decide the host for the counting endpoint and whether you will buy the products for photos. Everything else can stay at its default.

Nothing in this list needs a developer. Steps 1–3 are an afternoon; the automation is already written and waiting for the data they produce.
