Disclosure

Affiliate disclosure for The Countertop Guide. What the links are, how they are marked, and what we do and do not claim.

The short version

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Links marked Sponsored go to Amazon. If you buy through one, the site may earn a commission.

What we mark

  • Every Amazon product link carries `rel="sponsored"` (Google's guidance on paid links, SRC-09) and is labelled Sponsored where it appears.
  • Links to official sources, such as Amazon policy pages and government guidance, are not sponsored links.

Why this statement is here

Amazon's Associates Operating Agreement requires the statement "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." to be stated clearly and prominently on the site (SRC-01, §5). It appears under the masthead on every page and again in the footer.

The FTC says a disclosure is likely to be missed if it appears only on an About Me or profile page, at the end of a post, or anywhere that requires a person to click to see more. It should be placed so it is hard to miss (SRC-05). That is why the statement sits above the content on each page, not only here.

What we do not claim

  • No hands-on testing. We have not tested the products on this site. Guides cite listing text and labelled manufacturer claims.
  • No prices or star ratings. Participation Requirements §2(b) allows prices and availability only through an Amazon-served link or the Product Advertising API, and §6(t) allows reviews and star ratings only through the API (SRC-02).
  • No rankings or "best" claims. Each guide compares features that the listings state.
  • No revenue forecast. The site does not predict earnings.

Our links

The links the site uses are the ones supplied to us. Their compliance status is unresolved, and the tracking ID has not yet been tested in Associates Central. The status of each link is on the link validation page, and any change will be recorded there.
