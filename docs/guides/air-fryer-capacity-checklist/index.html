A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity numbers on air fryer listings can mean different things. This checklist shows what to check before you compare sizes.

By Editorial team · Published 2026-11-20 · Last checked 2026-11-20

Capacity is a number, and the number has a unit

Listings give capacity in quarts: "6 Quart," "8 Qt," "26QT." That tells you the size the maker quotes. It does not tell you how much food fits in one batch.

The checklist

  • Find out whether the number is the total or per basket. The Chefman 6 Quart Dual Basket Sponsored listing is titled "6 Quart Dual Basket" and also says "2 Independent 3Qt" baskets. Read the basket size, not only the headline number.
  • Count your real batch. Write down the largest amount you cook at one time. Compare that with the basket or interior size on the listing.
  • Look at the layout. Two smaller baskets hold different food from one large one. The Chefman 6 Quart Dual Basket Sponsored listing describes its baskets as "Independent."
  • Ask how the number was measured. The parts of the listing we read did not say how the figure was measured. If it matters, ask the seller or manufacturer.
  • Remember the counter. A bigger basket or oven body takes more room. The counter-space guide covers that.

What we did not check

We did not weigh, measure, or run any of these units. The capacity figures here come from listing titles only, and none is treated as verified.
