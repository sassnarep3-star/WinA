Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Shape decides how much room you need and what you can cook at once. Here is what the register shows for each shape, and what it does not show.

Start with the shape

Two of the five register rows are basket-style listings: P01 (CHEFMAN 6 Quart Dual Basket) and P03 (Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry basket). P02 is listed as a French Door toaster-oven style unit (Emeril Lagasse 26QT). The shape is taken from the listing title only.

Compare what the listing says

Listing titles give function counts: P02 says '24 One-Touch Preset Options'; P03 says '4-in-1'; P01 mentions 'Hi-Fry' and 'Sync Finish'. These are the seller's claims, not tested results. Check each claim on the live listing before you rely on it.

What this guide does not show

Cooking performance, cooking times, noise and footprint were not captured or tested. Those fields are blank in the register and are flagged.

Checklist

  • Which shape fits the counter you have (measure it; the register has no dimensions)
  • How many people you cook for, and whether you need two cooking zones at once
  • The listed functions, and which ones you would really use
  • Whether the accessories listed come with the unit
