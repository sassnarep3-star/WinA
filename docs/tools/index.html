Tools

Two calculators and a printable sheet

Everything on this page computes from what you type. We deliberately print no appliance figures of our own: there is no verified listing to take them from, and a made-up wattage would turn a useful tool into a lie with a decimal point.

What it costs to run

Type the wattage from the listing's own spec table and your supplier's rate per kWh. The arithmetic is printed next to the answer so you can check it rather than trust it.

Will it fit your counter

Appliance dimensions come from the listing; the gap is whatever your own manual demands around the unit. Enter both and see the space you actually have to clear.

What we did not check

  • We have not used, measured, or timed any appliance while writing this.
  • We print no price, no star rating, and no availability, because we have no verified listing to read them from.
  • Nothing here ranks anything. There is no "top pick", because a ranking needs a comparison set and we do not have one.
