Executive summary

The state of the project in one page — written to be read once and acted on. As of November 20, 2026.

1 · Situation

The Crisp Counter is live: a publication-style Amazon affiliate site for a five-product kitchen lineup (three air fryers, two cooking thermometers) promoted under tracking ID shopnow047c-20. It replaces the original "audit-style" brief with a shop a buyer can act on — while keeping the verification machinery reachable, automated where possible, and gated where not.

2 · What's live

Storefront: home, shop (5 products in 2 categories), and a full comparison table where every row is source-linked and unconfirmed cells are flagged, never guessed.

Editorial: four question-shaped buyer guides (form factor, capacity, cleanup/counter space, multi-function questions) — working topics, labeled as hypotheses, not popularity claims.

Decision support: a suggestions queue (13 proposals, nothing implemented without approval) and an open-questions register (16 questions with discovery paths).

Machinery: sitemap, RSS feed, robots.txt, five-event privacy-preserving analytics layer with a self-hosted endpoint ready to deploy, and a CI compliance gate (tools/check_site.py) that blocks undisclosed or broken pages from publishing.

4 · Risks & flags (published on the Flagged for Review page — none block the site)

FLG-001 submitted identifiers are 9-char tokens, not canonical ASINs — kept unmerged from observed targets. FLG-002 tracking attribution unconfirmed until one link per product is tested in Associates reports. FLG-003 submitted short links' compliance unresolved (neither endorsed nor condemned). FLG-004/007 two listing-internal inconsistencies documented, not silently fixed — and both re-read end to end on 2026-11-20: the 450°F is a named Hi-Fry option beside a stated 200–400°F range, and 6 QT is 5.67 L, so neither is a contradiction once the whole listing is read. What remains open is narrower: the Hi-Fry duration is published only on a different Chefman SKU (FLG-045), and the CHEF iQ model code recorded was the one-probe variant of a three-probe listing (FLG-046). FLG-005 thumbnails hot-linked pending PA-API licensing. FLG-006 one help URL may need a login.
