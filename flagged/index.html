FLG-003 review

Compliance status of link.amazon short links is unresolved

Whether link.amazon short links satisfy the requirement that a link not obscure the destination is not something we can settle from published policy text. Non-Amazon-owned shorteners are clearly addressed; this is Amazon's own domain. We are not claiming these links are invalid. The question is open.

What we did: Not depended upon. The site links directly to amazon.com product pages instead. Raised for review so the question gets a real answer from Associates support.

FLG-027 review

The 8-quart Chefman's 450°F was presented as its maximum temperature

The listing title says '450°F Hi-Fry Option'; the manufacturer states Hi-Fry 'raises the heat to 450°F for the last two minutes', against a normal range topping out at 400°F. This site had recorded 'Max temperature: 450°F (Hi-Fry)' and a guide called it 'the top of the range in this set'. That overstates a two-minute browning finish as a continuous capability.

What we did: Corrected. The row now reads '400°F standard range; 450°F Hi-Fry for the final 2 minutes of a cycle', and the basket-vs-oven guide describes it the same way.

FLG-011 action

FLG-013 action

Content volume is below the Associates review bar

Amazon's Associates Help, 'Application Review Process' (checked 2026-11-20, US help topic G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM), says: "All Sites must have robust original content (even when advertising is removed) – a good rule of thumb is at least 10 posts." It also says: "Content on your website must be recent (generally within the last 60 days)." This site has 5 guides and 5 product pages. Amazon does not say whether product and hub pages count as posts, but 5 guides is below the rule of thumb on any reading. The account's own status and review history are unknown from here. Carried forward from the parallel-session register (arena-2ca62ce9, FLG-010), which found this first.

What we did: Do not submit or re-submit for review until the site has at least 10 posts and content dated within the last 60 days. The weekly cadence in the suggestions register (SUG-05) is the proposed path. Recorded as a commercial blocker, not a content-quality one.

FLG-037 resolved

The structural gate's Amazon matcher skipped the two category browse links

scripts/check-site.mjs classified Amazon hrefs with a path pattern that required a trailing slash after /b/, but a category browse node is written /Air-Fryers/b?node=17659096011. The two category-hub browse links therefore sat outside the three-state rule that gate exists to enforce, and its total read 94 Amazon links while scripts/compliance-gate.mjs read 96 for the same tree. Both links happened to be correctly declared non-affiliate citations, so nothing was broken — but the gate that was written to catch an undeclared Amazon anchor would not have caught one of those two. The pattern now accepts /b/ and /b?, and both gates count the same 96 (80 sponsored).

What we did: Resolved. The matcher and this note were added together; if the two totals ever disagree again, treat it as a coverage gap rather than a rounding difference.

scripts/check-site.mjs, the independently written structural gate
