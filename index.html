Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

They share a name and almost nothing else. The decision is not about which fries better — it is about how you cook on a normal weeknight.

A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Quarts are the least honest number on the box. Here is what to check instead, and why the biggest number is often the wrong buy.

 03

How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

The appliance you stop using is the one that is annoying to wash. This is the least glamorous part of the decision and the most predictive.

 04

Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

Every appliance claims ten jobs. The useful question is which two it does well, and whether those two are the jobs you have.

 05

Instant-read or wireless: which meat thermometer fits your grill?

One answers "is this done right now?". The other answers "tell me when it is done" while the lid stays shut. The right one depends on how you cook.

 06

Air fryer or toaster oven: which one earns its counter space?

They brown food with moving hot air and they both sit on your counter like a houseguest who will not leave. The difference is what you can put in them and what you give up to have them.

Five questions to ask before buying any small kitchen appliance

Not air-fryer questions. Appliance questions. Ask them of a pocket thermometer and a 26-quart countertop oven alike, and most buyer regret disappears before you click buy.

Every specification on this site is marked with whether it is verified from the official listing, stated by the manufacturer but unverifiable, unverified, or withheld because Amazon's program policies do not allow us to display it.
