How these recommendations were made

We did not test these appliances. No page on this site claims otherwise, and it will not claim otherwise until someone actually does the testing. What we did instead was read the official listings carefully and write about the decision rather than a verdict: what each form factor is good at, what it is bad at, and who should not buy it. Every specification on this site is marked with whether it is verified from the official listing, stated by the manufacturer but unverifiable, unverified, or withheld because Amazon's program policies do not allow us to display it. Prices, star ratings, review counts and product photography are all absent for that last reason. That makes this site less useful than it could be. It also means nothing here is invented.
