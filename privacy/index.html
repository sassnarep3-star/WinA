Privacy

What is collected, what is not, and why the answer is currently closer to nothing than you might expect.

The short version

This site does not set cookies. It does not fingerprint your browser. It does not run third-party trackers, advertising pixels, or social embeds. There is no login and no account.

What is measured

Five events are defined for this site: a page view, an outbound click, a guide read, a comparison view, and an intent to subscribe. Each is designed to carry only the page path, an ASIN where relevant, and a coarse viewport size.

None of it is currently being recorded. No analytics collector is deployed, so the client is switched off rather than sending events into a void. This is documented on the measurement page.

If a collector is deployed later, the design is: first-party and self-hosted, no cookies, no identifiers, no cross-site tracking, raw events discarded after thirty days and only daily totals kept.

The email form

The subscribe form in the footer is not connected to any email service. If you submit it, nothing is transmitted, nothing is stored, and no address leaves your browser. The page says so at the point where you would find out the hard way.

What Amazon sees

When you click a product link, you leave this site and arrive at Amazon. From that moment Amazon's own privacy notice governs what happens, and it is a separate relationship from this one. The link carries our Associates tracking tag, which tells Amazon which site referred you. That is how the commission is attributed, and it is the whole purpose of the tag.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies of any kind.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts, or images loaded from other domains.
  • No advertising network, retargeting, or pixel.
  • No selling, sharing, or renting of any data, because none is collected.
  • No collection of payment information, ever.

Third parties

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which is a third party and will see standard web server information such as your IP address as part of delivering the page. That is a property of any web host and is outside this site's control. No analytics service, CDN of our own, or processor receives anything from this site.

If this changes

Any future addition of analytics, email, or third-party tooling would require this page to be rewritten before the tool goes live, and the change recorded. Amazon's program policies also require accurate disclosure of cookies, pixels and third-party technologies, so this page is a compliance document as well as a courtesy.

Contact and correction

There is no contact form, because a form would be another thing collecting data. Corrections are handled through the source register, which lists every claim and where it came from.
