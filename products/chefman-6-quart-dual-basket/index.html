Why it is on this list

  • Two independent 3-quart baskets let you cook a main and a side at different temperatures in one run.
  • Sync Finish is the feature that matters: it holds the faster basket so both finish together.
  • Easy-view windows mean you check browning without opening the drawer and dumping heat.
  • Both nonstick baskets are listed as dishwasher-safe, which is what actually decides whether you keep using it.

What to think twice about

  • 6 quarts total is a real limitation for a family of four cooking one-basket meals; it is two 3-quart cooks, not one 6-quart cook.
  • Dual-basket bodies are wider than single-basket models of the same capacity. Measure your counter before you buy, not after.

Who it is wrong for

Anyone cooking one large thing. Two 3-quart drawers cannot hold a whole chicken, and 6 quarts split in half is less usable volume than it sounds. If you consistently cook a single big batch, the 8-quart single basket does that better and with less washing. It is also wrong for anyone whose counter cannot spare the extra width a dual-basket body needs.
