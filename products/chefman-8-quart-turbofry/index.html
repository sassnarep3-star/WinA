Why it is on this list

  • A single 8-quart drawer is easier to fill, toss, and wash than two smaller ones when you are cooking one thing for everyone.
  • Hi-Fry adds a 450°F burst for the final two minutes of a cook, which is a browning finish rather than a full 450°F cycle.
  • Bake, dehydrate and frozen modes stretch a single appliance across more of the week.
  • Nonstick basket is listed as dishwasher-safe and the body is stainless steel.

What to think twice about

  • One drawer means one temperature at a time. If your meals are protein plus side, you will be running it twice or holding food warm.
  • 8 quarts of food in one basket crowds if you fill it. Air frying needs space around the food; a full basket steams instead of crisps.
  • No viewing window is listed, so checking means pulling the drawer.

Who it is wrong for

Anyone whose meals are a protein and a side at different temperatures. One drawer means one temperature, so you will cook in sequence or compromise. It is also wrong if you want to bake in a pan or cook something whole and flat.
