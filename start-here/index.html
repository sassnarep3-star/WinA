I cook for two, most nights

You want one chamber, one temperature, one basket to wash. The dual basket is the wrong shape here — you would be heating an empty drawer to cook one piece of protein. Of the single-chamber fryers, the 8-quart is the only one, so it wins on shape rather than on capacity.

My counter is tiny

Measure before you buy, because the biggest number is the wrong buy if it has to live next to the toaster every day. The published footprint of the dual basket is 13.38 × 11.81 × 10.63 inches and the French-door oven is 16.9 × 15.8 × 14.8. The 8-quart fryer's dimensions are not published, which is itself a reason to ask before you commit.
