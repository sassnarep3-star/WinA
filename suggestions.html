Suggestions queue — decide before we build

Everything below is a proposal, not a change. Each card states its evidence basis, effort, risk, and the path we recommend — with the alternative kept visible. Reply with a proposal ID and a verdict (approve / reject / revise) and only then does it enter the build.

How to use this queue

1. Skim the verdict we recommend. 2. Check the evidence line — "verified" means it's backed by a linked source; "hypothesis" means it's plausible but unmeasured; "blocked" means it can't proceed until a question is answered. 3. Reply e.g. "S-03 approve, S-08 revise: skip the video". Nothing is implemented without that step. Changes already mandated by the project brief are listed at the bottom as shipped, so this queue stays purely about decisions that are still open.

Look and feel

S-01

Give each product a short "editor's note" framing who it fits

status: awaiting decision
Why
Shops that convert read like a knowledgeable friend. Short neutral framing ("best if you cook two things at once") speeds decisions without fake reviews.
Evidence
Hypothesis — conversion effect is unmeasured until the campaign ledger has data.
Recommended
Approve with guardrails: framing must never assert performance we haven't verified; each note ends with a link to its facts.
Alternative
Keep cards spec-only (current state) — safest, slower to read.
Effort / risk
Low / low (editorial risk only, managed by the compliance gate G-5 review).
S-02

Add "price history" sparklines per product

status: awaiting decision
Why
Deal badges are everywhere; a price-history strip would differentiate and help buyers.
Evidence
Blocked — needs a lawful, first-party price observation cadence. We currently have one observation date (2026-11-20). See Q-11.
Recommended
Defer until at least 30 daily observations exist, then revisit.
Alternative
Show only "last observed" + date (current state) — honest and static.
Effort / risk
Medium / medium (stale charts mislead; needs automation + review).
S-03

Evidence
Verified timing (listing return policies, 2026-11-20) + hypothesis on traffic.
Recommended
Approve a pilot: one "Holiday hosting gift guide" before Dec 10, added to sitemap + feed.
Alternative
Keep seasonal advice as a banner on the home page (current state).
Effort / risk
Low / low.
S-04

Replace hot-linked product thumbnails with PA-API images

S-05

Question-shaped topic cluster: "Air fryer mistakes"

status: awaiting decision
Why
Common-search question shapes ("why is my air fryer not crispy") match how buyers look for help; the four current guides cover pre-purchase questions, not post-purchase ones.
Evidence
Hypothesis — per the brief, the next topic should be chosen from measured queries once Search Console data exists (Q-12).
Recommended
Hold until 2–4 weeks of query data exist, then pick from real queries. The brief's own principle: measure before optimizing.
Alternative
Publish now on instinct (against the brief's evidence-first rule).
Effort / risk
Medium / low.

Verified confusion exists in the source data itself (FLG-004, FLG-007).
Recommended

Alternative
Inline tooltips only — less findable.
Effort / risk
Low / low.
S-07

Real launch list (email capture with consent + double opt-in)

status: awaiting decision
Why
Reader capture is the one growth loop that survives search-algorithm changes. The current form is an honest no-op (see measurement).
Evidence
Hypothesis on value; verified that a compliant design needs a real processor and consent language.
Recommended
Approve in principle, pick a processor: self-hosted list (privacy-aligned, more ops) vs hosted provider (faster, adds a processor to the privacy policy). Decide via Q-13.
Alternative
RSS-only (current state) — zero compliance surface, slower growth.
Effort / risk
Medium / medium (privacy obligations once emails are stored).

Comparison page: add "what we'd test" columns instead of scores

Readers want verdicts; scoring without testing is the classic affiliate-site sin. "What we'd test" columns (noise, preheat, evenness) tell the truth and set us apart.
Evidence
Verified: no test data exists for these products (methodology).
Recommended
Approve. Cheap to add; strengthens the trust story.
Alternative
Leave the table as-is.
Effort / risk
Low / low.

S-10

Weekly automated re-verification of prices and stock

status: awaiting decision
Why
Observed prices age within days; stale prices damage trust (and Amazon's policies expect accuracy). A weekly check updates the "observed" stamps automatically.
Evidence
Verified risk: all prices here are a single 2026-11-20 snapshot.
Recommended
Approve after G-4 (tooling should use official APIs where required). Until then, manual monthly re-observation.
Alternative
Remove prices entirely and link only — less useful, always accurate.
Effort / risk
Medium / medium (rate limits, automation policy — see gate G-7).

Approve (repo settings change — one click).

Keep it advisory (current state).

Minimal / low.

S-13

Link-out gate modal on first sponsored click per session

status: awaiting decision
Why
An interstitial ("this is a sponsored link to Amazon, tracking unconfirmed — details") maximizes disclosure conspicuity while tracking is in pilot state (FLG-002).
Evidence
Hypothesis on trust benefit; verified friction cost (modals suppress clicks).
Recommended

Alternative
Do nothing (current state — already compliant per gate G-1).
Effort / risk
Low / medium (UX friction).
S-14

Decide the price/rating display posture

status: awaiting owner decision
Why
Two builds of this site disagree on one compliance judgment: this site shows dated, source-linked observed prices/ratings (flagged FLG-009); the parallel build cites Associates Program Policies (updated 2026-04-14) §6 and shows no prices at all.
Evidence
Verbatim policy clauses and citations in data/policy.json (POL-03/POL-04); the conservative posture is live in the archived parallel build.
Recommended
Owner decides — buyer usefulness vs policy caution. Record the choice on the compliance page and clear FLG-009 either way.
Alternative
Hybrid: keep prices in research tables but suppress star ratings until PA-API access exists.
Effort / risk
Low effort to switch / high value of certainty.
