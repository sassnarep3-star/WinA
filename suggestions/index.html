SUG-05 Publish on a real cadence: a new guide every week for eight weeks, then read the query data recommend 8 Question-shaped guides are the right shape, but five is not a sample. A weekly cadence for two months produces enough pages and enough query impressions to see which question people actually arrive with, which is the only way to stop guessing topics. A substantive guide per week. Each needs its own verified spec pass. Cadence without quality control produces the thin content the brief explicitly rejects. The build gate must stay on. How to review and choose. Score each suggestion, read the risk, then accept or reject in writing.
StepRule
1Answer the blocking questions first (Q-04 enrolment, Q-09 testing, Q-16 measurement).
2Score each suggestion: impact × confidence ÷ cost.
3Read the risk column before accepting; a suggestion that needs a credential is gated, not blocked.
4Accept or reject in writing, with a one-line reason and a date.
5Re-review quarterly against measured data, not instinct.

Kill criteria

  • A topic with no measured demand after four weeks is dropped, not padded.
  • A suggestion whose risk has grown (for example it would breach policy) is moved to rejected.
  • No new topic is added from instinct while the query report is empty.
