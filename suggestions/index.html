Suggestions

A decision list, not a wish list. Each suggestion states what to do, why, what it costs, and what would make it a bad idea. Rows marked Shipped are live on this site; the three paths at the top say what order to do the rest in. Where two sessions proposed opposite moves — a seasonal page now (SUG-25) versus held (SUG-23), original drawings (SUG-30) versus generated scenes (SUG-27) — both rows stay, and the decision log says which one this build took.

Choose a path, not a wish

Three coherent ways forward, in order. Each is a sequence, not a menu: a step is only worth doing once the step before it is done, and steps marked blocked are blocked on an account, not on a decision we can make here. The pick is recorded so the choice is explicit.

Design Counter Space, a polished marketing affiliate publication about kitchen gear. Visual direction: an opinionated editorial type system — serif display headlines, clean sans body, generous line height, readable measure. Restrained palette with one accent color; no blue-to-purple gradients, no neon highlights, no emoji or decorative icons. Vary section layouts — a strong editorial hero, a reading column for guides, at least one non-card treatment per page; do not repeat the identical card grid everywhere. Keep the disclosure strip above the fold on every page and the Sponsored label on every outbound product link; these are compliance requirements, not afterthoughts. Write in a concrete, human voice using specifics and named trade-offs; ban filler phrases and generic marketing boilerplate. Until compliant product imagery is available, keep the site typographic: color, type and an illustration or pattern system carry the design. Do not hot-link, store, or substitute Amazon imagery, and do not use stock photos of a different appliance. Meet WCAG contrast and stay readable on mobile, especially tables and cards. HTML/CSS only; no third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

Constraints to design against:

  • FTC Disclosures 101 — disclosure hard to miss, placed with the endorsement
  • Associates Operating Agreement §5 — verbatim Associate statement above the fold
  • Program Policies §6(a) and IP License — no unlicensed or misrepresenting imagery
  • FTC 16 CFR 255.5 — sponsored status visible at the link
  • Google Search Central helpful-content — concrete human voice, no filler

How to review and choose. Score each suggestion, read the risk, then accept or reject in writing.
StepRule
1Answer the blocking questions first (Q-04 enrolment, Q-09 testing, Q-16 measurement).
2Score each suggestion: impact × confidence ÷ cost.
3Read the risk column before accepting; a suggestion that needs a credential is gated, not blocked.
4Accept or reject in writing, with a one-line reason and a date.
5Re-review quarterly against measured data, not instinct.

Kill criteria

  • A topic with no measured demand after four weeks is dropped, not padded.
  • A suggestion whose risk has grown (for example it would breach policy) is moved to rejected.
  • No new topic is added from instinct while the query report is empty.
