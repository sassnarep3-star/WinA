SUG-18 Ask each manufacturer for the interior cavity dimensions — the one figure nobody publishes recommend 9 Every capacity guide on this site currently ends in a gap: exterior dimensions are published, interior dimensions are not, and "can a whole chicken fit" cannot be answered from them. Record whatever they answer with its date and its source, exactly like every other specification. Recommend. Do it before the next capacity guide is written, and record the answer in data/products.json with its source rather than in prose.
SUG-25 Do not publish year-stamped "best of" listicles reject 9 This is the single most recognisable pattern of a generated affiliate site, and the brief explicitly asks for something that does not read like one. A "Best Air Fryers of 2026" list implies a tested, ranked, current verdict; this site has neither the tests nor the prices, and the year in the title guarantees decay. It also invites the copy-paste structure that every competitor publishes, which is not a strategy. None. This is a prohibition, not a build. The argument for it is that it is what ranks. It is what ranks in the short term, and it is what produces the pages that get penalised and abandoned. Reject, permanently. Listed here so that a future session does not add one in good faith. The question-shaped guides are the deliberate alternative.
SUG-05 Publish on a real cadence: one guide a week for eight weeks, then read the query data recommend 8 Question-shaped guides are the right shape, but four is not a sample. A weekly cadence for two months produces enough pages and enough query impressions to see which question people actually arrive with, which is the only way to stop guessing topics. One substantive guide per week. Each needs its own verified spec pass. Cadence without quality control produces the thin content the brief explicitly rejects. The build gate must stay on. Partly done, deliberately. Twelve guides now exist, but they were published in one pass, which is not a cadence. The weekly rhythm is still the plan, and the next topic should be chosen from the query report once one exists. Do not mistake a bigger library for a cadence.
SUG-06 Add a real author byline with a named person recommend 8 Unattributed buying advice reads as generated, which is precisely the failure the brief names. A byline with a short bio and a stated method is the cheapest credibility signal available. Requires a real person willing to be named and accountable. A byline attached to content the person did not write is worse than no byline. Decision needed from the requester. Cannot be fabricated.

SUG-23 Commission one hands-on test, properly disclosed, rather than guessing at everything recommend 8 This is the unlock the whole site is waiting on. Every guide currently ends at the edge of a measured fact: preheat time, how much a basket browns, whether the crumb tray is awkward, what the 450°F Hi-Fry burst actually does to a chicken wing. One tested unit per form factor would convert an honest inference site into an evidence site, and it would end the awkwardness of writing about an appliance nobody has ever switched on. Three units, or one borrowed per category. Plus the discipline to write only what was measured. Testing a single unit invites over-generalisation, and a disclosed test must be disclosed on every page that cites it. Both are manageable; neither is a reason not to test. Recommend, and treat it as the real content strategy rather than a compliance nicety. Until it happens, the site stays in the "how to decide" voice, which is the correct voice for zero tests.

If you accept only three

SUG-04 — prove one affiliate link actually tracks, because nothing else matters if it does not. SUG-03 — get API access, which unlocks prices, ratings and images at once. SUG-23 — test one unit per form factor, which is the only thing that turns this from an inference site into an evidence site.

Three suggestions are rejections

SUG-12 (price tracking), SUG-13 (generated near-duplicate pages) and SUG-25 (year-stamped "best of" listicles) are listed so that a future session does not build them in good faith. The first breaches Amazon's program policies outright; the second and third produce the exact content this publication exists to avoid.
