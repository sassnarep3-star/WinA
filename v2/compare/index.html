How to read this table

Start from the field that constrains you, not from the rating. If two people are cooking at once, the dual-basket column matters more than half a star. If you are feeding six, the capacity column matters more than the mode count. If you keep overcooking chicken, the thermometer columns matter more than any of the fryer columns.

A dash means the field does not apply to that kind of product. “Not confirmed” means the listing did not state it and we refused to fill the gap.
