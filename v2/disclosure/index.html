Disclosure

The short version appears under the masthead of every page on this site. This is the long version.

The statement

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

That is the exact wording the Associates Program Operating Agreement (§5) requires, and it appears above the fold on every page, repeated in every footer. It is not only on this page.

What that means in practice

  • When you click a product link and buy something, the seller pays us a small percentage. The price you pay is unchanged.
  • We do not know who you are. We receive aggregate counts, not identities.
  • Nothing on this site is a paid placement. No brand paid us to be listed, ranked, described or omitted.

Why there are no prices here

Amazon's Associates Program Policies permit a site to show price and availability only if (a) Amazon serves the link in which that data is displayed, or (b) the data comes from the Creators API or PA API and complies with the license. This is a static site. It satisfies neither condition, so it prints no price at all. That is a rule we are following, not an oversight.

Source: Associates Program Policies, updated 2026-04-14, Participation Requirements §2(b).

Why every product link is marked sponsored

Every outbound link to Amazon on this site carries rel="sponsored". The FTC's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255, §255.5) require that a material connection be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. Marking the link as sponsored and stating the connection in plain words, in the place where the reader encounters the link, is how this site does that.

Source: 16 CFR Part 255, current as of 2026-11-18.

What we do not do

  • We do not claim to have tested a product we have not held. No page on this site says “we tested”.
  • We do not invent reviews, quotes or star ratings. The only ratings shown are Amazon's, stamped with the date we read them.
  • We do not print a specification we could not source. Those cells say “Not confirmed”.
  • We do not collect personal data. No cookies are set and analytics ships disabled.
