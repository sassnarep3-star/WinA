The five events

Written, shipped, and switched off. When an endpoint is chosen, these begin flowing with no code change. Nothing here identifies a reader.

Privacy position. No cookies, no localStorage identifiers, no fingerprinting, no third-party requests, no cross-site tracking. Events are sent to the site owner's own endpoint and are never shared with Amazon or anyone else. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured by not sending anything.

EventFires whenWhy it existsFieldsFeeds
disclosure_view The disclosure strip first enters the viewport on any page. Proves the material connection was actually on screen, not merely present in the DOM. This is the compliance-evidence event. page session_bucket S01, S07
product_view A product page or product card becomes the primary item in view. Tells you which of the five products people actually reach, independent of what they click. product_id asin page session_bucket ledger.source
outbound_click A click on any link carrying rel="sponsored" that resolves to amazon.com. The outbound-click counter. This is the only click number this site can measure on its own; reported orders must come from Amazon. product_id asin link_id page placement session_bucket ledger.outbound_clicks
compare_open A reader opens or expands a comparison table. Distinguishes readers who are still deciding from readers who have decided. Useful for choosing the next guide topic. page comparison_id session_bucket editorial decision input (Q11)
guide_read_complete A reader scrolls past 90% of a guide's body. The only honest signal that a guide was actually read rather than bounced off. guide_id page session_bucket publishing cadence input (SG-06)

Session bucketing without tracking

A random 6-character value generated in memory per page load. It is not persisted and not stored in any cookie or storage API. It exists only to group events within one page view and cannot be joined across pages or across visits.

Prevents counting one reader's five scroll events as five readers.
