What the register does and does not prove

It proves that all five short links resolve to the ASIN the owner described. Every row is verifiable by following the link yourself.

It does not prove that those links credit the tracking ID. None of the observed redirect targets contains a tag= parameter; they carry Amazon's own page-share token instead. We are not claiming the links are invalid. We are recording that their compliance status is unresolved and cannot be settled from outside the account.

The cheapest way to settle it is one click, followed by one look at the Associates click report. That is logged as Q03 and as the first item in the decision queue.
