Who it suits

Batch cooking for four to six people who want one large chamber and one thing to wash.

Where it gives something up

  • One chamber means one temperature at a time. A crisp side and a slow main cannot run simultaneously.
  • The 400°F ceiling is lower than the 450°F Hi-Fry finish, which is a short final stage rather than a full cook temperature.
